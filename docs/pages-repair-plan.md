> **Status: done.** The plan below was carried out. The one place where it

> turned out to be far richer than the factory maps, so most of the targets
> classified as "not remotable in Reason" were real parameters under a

Companion to `docs/pages-audit.md`. Generated by
`remote/scripts/audit-pages.ps1` and `remote/scripts/classify-targets.ps1`
against `remote/mapping/reason-targets.json`, which is extracted from Reason's
factory maps by `remote/scripts/extract-targets.ps1`.

## What changed since the first audit

The first pass reported "205 invalid targets" but could not say whether they
were typos or genuinely unavailable. Two things were fixed in the extractor:

1. **LCD-driving surface maps are now skipped.** Mackie Control/Extender,
   M-Audio ProjectMix and Frontier Tranzport put literal screen captions in
   the target column (`"Faders "`, `  <  `) and surface-state selectors
   (`Display=ParaName`, `Flip=Flipped`). These are not device parameters.
2. **Variation selectors are now skipped** — `Knobs=`, `Mixer_vars=`,
   `Send_vars=`, `Variation=`, `Parameters=`. These switch which parameter
   bank a control addresses; they are not remotable items.

After that, the 205 figure held — so it is not a parsing artefact. It is real.

So many template targets are **not typos but parameters that Reason does not
expose**. There is nothing to rename them to.

### D. Scope not installed / Player devices — cannot validate (3)

`morfin.yaml` (`com.groovymelon.MorfinXF`), `note-echo.yaml`
(`se.propellerheads.NoteEcho`) and `scales-and-chords.yaml`
(`se.propellerheads.ScalesAndChords`) name scopes that appear nowhere in the
factory maps. Note Echo and Scales & Chords are Reason **Player** devices,
which only register a scope when a song containing them is open. These three
templates should be marked unvalidated rather than fixed speculatively.

## The paging model — decided

The templates describe a 6-page mixer (Mixer, EQ, Dynamics, Sends 1-4,
Sends 5-8, Insert FX) with PermaBar keys `Mix/User/Instr/Learn/View/FX`.

The shipped, hardware-verified map is **4 pages** (Mixer, Dynamics, EQ, FX)
with PermaBar keys `Mix/User/Instr/FX`.

**The 4-page model wins.** The templates must be brought to it, not the other
way round. Consequences for `reason-main-mixer-channel.yaml`:

* Sends 1-4, Sends 5-8 and Insert FX collapse into one FX page.
* Only PermaBar keys Mix/User/Instr/FX are navigation; Learn and View revert
  to spare (available for LED/context phase later).
* Page assignments follow the shipped map: `User`→Dynamics, `Instr`→EQ.
